# Council of Aru'Mas

The Council of Aru'Mas is the city's governing assembly. It is not a body of three seats, as the popular
shorthand has it, nor a chamber open to every interested party. It is thirteen delegates, drawn from three
distinct bodies, each of which selects its representatives by its own rules. Power in Aru'Mas is shared not
between three people but between three constituencies, and the arithmetic of that sharing is deliberate.

---

## Composition

### The Crown (3 seats)

- **One member of the Royal Family.** Generally the ruling monarch, currently King [[Voren Ilderas]].
  The seat may be filled by another member of the family in the monarch's stead as circumstances require.
- **Two delegates from the Noble Houses.** These are not permanent appointments.
  The houses represented, and the individuals sent, are chosen by the ruling monarch before each meeting,
  and depend largely on the matter under discussion. A session on harbour tariffs and a session on wall
  maintenance will rarely seat the same two nobles.

The rotating noble seats are the Crown's most flexible instrument and its most contested one. The monarch
does not merely hold one vote; the monarch decides, meeting by meeting, which two houses get to hold the
other two. Houses court the Crown accordingly.

### The Scepter (3 seats)

One representative for each branch of the Pathite Faith:

- The **High Warden**, for the [[Order of Valor]]
- The **High Oracle**, for the [[Order of Wisdom]]
- The **High Hierophant**, for the [[Order of Harmony]]

The **Arbiter** holds no seat. [[High Priest Alabaster Sayn|The Arbiter]] is the voice of consensus
between [[Alor]], [[Orenn]] and [[Vesira]], and stands above the three Order heads within the Faith, but
he does not sit in the chamber and does not vote. His will is spoken for by the three heads who do.

### The People (7 seats)

- **Three delegates from the [[Merchants Guild|Merchants' Guild]]**, led by Grand Speaker
  [[Grand Speaker Artrice Vanderbane|Artrice Vanderbane]].
- **Four delegates from the [[Adventurers Guild Association]].**

The Adventurers Guild Association holds the largest single delegation on the Council. In a city whose
founding treaty restricts access to the archipelago, and whose economy runs on licensed passage through
that restriction, the four seats reflect what the adventuring population is actually worth to Aru'Mas.

---

## The Transcendent Voice

The Council is chaired by the **Transcendent Voice**, ambassador to Aru'Mas from the King of Spirits.

The Voice speaks for the spiritual community of the archipelago. He is not permitted a vote and holds no
delegation, but he may address the Council at any point, without recognition or invitation, to make the
will of the spirits known. He also conducts the meeting itself, impartially, which places the procedural
running of the city's governing body in the hands of a party with no stake in any of its factions and no
power to decide anything it does.

The arrangement is not ceremonial. A city whose legal existence rests on the [[First Pact]] keeps the
spirits' ambassador in the room where the Pact could be broken.

---

## Procedure

A fully staffed session seats **thirteen voting delegates** from **three bodies** across **seven
organisations**: the Royal Family, the Noble Houses, the Order of Valor, the Order of Wisdom, the Order of
Harmony, the Merchants' Guild, and the Adventurers Guild Association.

**Consequential measures require nine votes.** The supermajority produces a specific and well-understood
political shape:

| Grouping | Votes | Can carry a measure alone? | Can block one alone? |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Crown | 3 | No | No |
| The Scepter | 3 | No | No |
| The People | 7 | No | Yes |
| Merchants' Guild | 3 | No | No |
| Adventurers Guild Association | 4 | No | No (needs one ally) |
| Crown and Scepter together | 6 | No | Yes |

Two consequences follow, and both are understood by everyone who has ever sat in the chamber. First, **no
measure passes without at least two votes from the People**, because the remaining six delegates cannot
reach nine between them. Second, **the People cannot pass anything alone either**, because seven is not
nine. Every consequential act of government in Aru'Mas is therefore a coalition between the People and at
least one of the older bodies.

This is why the Crown's power to seat the two noble delegates matters so much. It is also why the
relationship between the Merchants' Guild and the Adventurers Guild Association is the most closely watched
in the city: together they hold a blocking majority, and apart they hold very little.
