# The Oasis of Spirits

The Oasis of Spirits is the lush park at the center of the [[Aurora Song District]], and one of the four subdistricts the district is divided into, alongside Artisan's Alley, the Performers' Quarter and the Inventor's Haven. Those three are built up with workshops, studios, galleries and theaters. The Oasis holds flowering trees, a pair of fountains and an open-air stage, with the workshops, theaters, galleries and vendors' streets of the district standing around it. It serves the district as both a sanctuary and a stage.

## The Dual Fountains

A pair of fountains stands in the park. They are read as a figure for the harmony between the material and the spiritual worlds, and are visited as a source of inspiration and wonder.

## The Green Amphitheater

An open-air stage ringed by flowering trees. Performances are held there nightly, and they draw audiences from across [[Aru'Mas]] rather than from the Aurora Song District alone. In the evening the Oasis as a whole becomes a stage for performances, with the district's taverns and theaters filling at the same hour.

## Day and Night

The park's two uses are separated by the clock. In the morning it is a quiet haven, where artists sketch and musicians rehearse in its corners while the district's workshops open and the street vendors set up their stalls. After dark, lanterns are lit and the Oasis becomes a gathering place filled with music and laughter.

## Place in the District

The city's record names the Oasis and the [[Horizon Watch Arena]] as the two points the district's strong adventuring culture concentrates around.

Aurora Song is the largest district in Aru'Mas by area and the liveliest in character, and it is also the district where the contradictions of the city show most plainly, with genuine creative energy on one street and sharp poverty on the next. The Oasis sits at the center of that.

## Connections
- [[Aurora Song District]] — the district the park sits at the center of, and one of whose four subdistricts it is
- [[Aru'Mas]] — the city whose record places the district's adventuring culture around the Oasis and the Horizon Watch Arena
- [[Horizon Watch Arena]] — named alongside the Oasis as the second focus of the district's adventuring culture
