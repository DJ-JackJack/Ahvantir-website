# Sailor's Row

Sailor's Row is a neighborhood of inns, taverns, and boarding houses in [[Driftmoor Haven]], where sailors, travelers, and adventurers can find a place to rest. It is a vital part of the district's economy.

## Beds and Boards

The houses here are not of one kind. At one end are the loud taverns, full of shouting and sea shanties. At the other are quiet inns kept for travelers who want a respite from the chaos of the docks. Boarding houses fill the ground between.

Two establishments are named in the sources. The Drowned Compass is a tavern frequented by adventurers and ship crews alike, and a favored tavern of [[Tarran Finn]], who often meets new arrivals to [[Aru'Mas]] there. The Restless Tide Inn takes visiting captains and merchants seeking discreet meetings and secret negotiations.

## The Black Market

Less scrupulous merchants work the Row, selling rare and illegal goods to those willing to pay the price.

The [[Runeguard]] keep a watchful eye on the Row and do not shut it down. Their restraint is practical rather than lenient. The Row is a vital part of the district's economy, and the Runeguard are careful not to disrupt the flow of commerce too much.

## The District Around It

Policing in Driftmoor Haven falls to the Watchmen of the Docks, a branch of the City Guard trained for the district. They work with the Port Authority, the Merchants' Guild, and the Runeguard to keep order and settle disputes.

The [[Dragon's Claws]] control smuggling routes through Driftmoor Haven's docks, and many dockworkers and merchants pay them for protection.
