# Far Farewell Trade Co-op

The Far Farewell Trade Co-op is a consignment market standing at the northern edge of the [[Far-Farewell Market District]] in [[Driftmoor Haven]]. It is indoors, with a sprawling interior divided into a labyrinth of booths, shelves and displays managed by independent sellers. Merchants, artisans and collectors all take space on the floor. The Co-op is counted among Driftmoor Haven's key locations, with the [[Merchants Guild Grand Hall]], the [[Many Worlds Pot and Barrel]] and [[Kragg’s Menagerie of Marvels]].

## The Floor

Stock runs from rare curios and handcrafted wares to plain everyday goods. The building is sectioned by type of goods: clothing in one part, tools in another, then books, then magical trinkets. The arrangement is meant to keep visitors walking. Each booth answers to its own owner. The Co-op is known for its mixed character and for finds nobody expected to make there, and is as much a community space as a marketplace.

## The Revenue Share

Sellers trade under a revenue-sharing arrangement, which encourages vendors to work with one another. It draws local entrepreneurs and travellers looking to sell or trade their wares.

A small cafe sits at the centre of the floor, with refreshments for visitors and somewhere to sit partway through a circuit of the booths.

## House Ninetti

[[House Ninetti]] counts the Co-op among the house's allied organisations. Those connections are held to give the Ninetti a voice in nearly every major import, export, and commercial policy decision made in [[Aru'Mas]].
