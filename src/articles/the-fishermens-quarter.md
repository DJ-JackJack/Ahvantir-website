# The Fishermen's Quarter

The Fishermen's Quarter is the part of [[Driftmoor Haven]] where local fishermen bring in their daily catch from the waters surrounding [[Aru'Mas]]. It is quieter than the Main Pier, which is the largest and busiest section of the district.

## The Catch

The catch comes off local boats and is sold on by vendors in the Quarter. The air holds the smell of fresh fish, the clatter of crates and the cries of vendors. What they sell runs from silver-scaled tuna to multiversal eels with glowing stripes.

## Who Lives Here

Many of the city's poorer residents live and work in the Quarter. It is a modest part of Driftmoor Haven and an essential one.

## Where the Rumours Start

Local rumour often starts here, as fishermen swap stories of strange happenings in the waters and the occasional sighting of sea spirits.

## Connections
- [[Driftmoor Haven]] — the port district the Quarter belongs to
- [[Aru'Mas]] — the city whose surrounding waters supply the catch
- [[Far-Farewell Market District]] — another part of Driftmoor Haven, an outdoor bazaar for goods from across the multiverse
