# The Performers' Quarter

The Performers' Quarter is the music, theater and dance quarter of the [[Aurora Song District]], one of that district's four subdistricts. Melodies or applause seem to reach most corners of the quarter.

## Venues

The Gilded Stage is the quarter's amphitheater and takes the work that needs room: plays, operas and magical displays staged at scale. It is a renowned house.

At the Wandering Minstrel Tavern, musicians and storytellers perform in exchange for meals and drinks.

## The Street Trade

Between the halls, street performers draw their own crowds. Street performers and vendors commonly accept Copper Chips, the lowest denomination of the [[Currency of Aru'Mas|city's coinage]].

## The Wider District

The district draws actors, dancers, singers and bards looking for an audience. [[The Gilded Bastion]] in [[Crowns Approach]] takes many of the performers who grace its halls from the Aurora Song District, for operas and for staged reenactments of the city's history. Performers of the district have put [[Friar Johan Veitr]] into plays and songs, often as a figure of comic resolve.

## Trouble

Aurora Song is the largest district in [[Aru'Mas]] by area and the liveliest in character, and it is also where the city's contradictions are most visible, with genuine creative energy in one street and sharp poverty in the next. The Quarter sits inside that. The [[Dragon's Claws]] keep ties to corrupt performers, black-market artisans and underground gambling operations across the district, and stand as silent patrons of illegal dueling rings and smuggled enchanted instruments.
