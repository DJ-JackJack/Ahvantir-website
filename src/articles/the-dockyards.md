# The Dockyards

The Dockyards are the repair quarter of [[Driftmoor Haven]], the stretch of the port where ships of all kinds are brought in for repairs, maintenance, and magical enhancement.

## The Trades

Three trades work the quarter: shipwrights, blacksmiths, and arcane engineers. They work together to see that the vessels leaving [[Aru'Mas]] are seaworthy and prepared for the challenges of multiversal travel.

## Arcane Shipbuilding

The quarter is known for its innovation in shipbuilding, particularly the integration of arcane technology into traditional vessels. Many ships leave the Dockyards equipped with enchanted hulls, magical sails, or runic cannons capable of fending off mundane and magical threats alike.

## Work and Crews

Ship crews in the Dockyards are always on the lookout for able-bodied recruits, either to help with the repairs or to join the crew for the next voyage. Sailors and adventurers seeking work come here for that reason.
