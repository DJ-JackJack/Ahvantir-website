# Orevicerium

Orevicerium is the name given to fragments of [[The Ranjergon|the Divine Gate]] torn free during the Divinity Conflict — the sole recorded instance in which the Gate was partially breached. The material does not occur naturally and cannot be manufactured. Every piece of Orevicerium in existence is a remnant of that single event.

The fragments retain traces of the Gate's original function. What this means in practical terms is debated by scholars of planar theology and arcane materials alike: some argue the retention is passive — that the material simply carries residual metaphysical charge — while others hold that Orevicerium fragments are, in some diminished and incoherent way, still active expressions of the Gate's purpose.

What is agreed upon is rarity and significance. Orevicerium is among the most historically and spiritually significant materials ever recovered in the mortal world. Its provenance connects it directly to [[The Ranjergon|the Divine War]], the birth of the Divine Gate, and the act of [[Imwei, the Green Mother]] that ended the age of direct divine presence in Ahvantir. To possess a fragment is to hold a piece of the boundary between the mortal world and the divine.

The Divinity Conflict itself — the event during which these fragments were scattered — is referenced in theological records as the one known occasion on which the Gate's absolute restriction was functionally compromised. The nature of that conflict, who instigated it, and what it achieved are questions with incomplete answers in the surviving record.
