# Artisan's Alley

Artisan's Alley is one of the four subdistricts of the [[Aurora Song District]] in [[Aru'Mas]]: a maze of workshops, studios, and galleries where the city's most skilled craftsmen and artists create their work and display it.

## Notable Features

**The Woven Canvas.** A public gallery holding rotating collections of paintings, sculptures, and experimental art. It shows work by artists resident in the city and by visitors passing through.

**The Craftsmen's Market.** A weekly gathering at which the quarter's artisans sell handmade goods directly. What appears ranges from intricate jewelry to custom-forged weapons, each piece showing its maker's personal style.

## Work and Atmosphere

The hum of tools and the splash of paint fill the air, with the murmur of collaboration beneath them. The Alley is a place of tireless work, with projects taking shape under the hands of their creators.

## Trade and Representation

Artisans and tradespeople across Aru'Mas often prefer payment in Silver Lions, worth ten Copper Chips and suited to medium-value purchases such as clothing, tools, and professional services. Commissioned art sits a tier above, in Gold Crowns, alongside luxury goods and the funding of minor projects ([[Currency of Aru'Mas]]). The city's artisan guild training covers textile weaving, glassblowing, smithing, and other crafts worked with magical elements ([[Education System of Aru'Mas]]).

Politically, craftspeople are represented by the Artisans' Council, a sub-body of the [[Merchants Guild]] covering blacksmiths, weavers, alchemists, and other skilled trades. The Guild holds three of the thirteen seats on the [[Council of Aru'Mas]], and [[Grand Speaker Artrice Vanderbane]] rose through the Artisans' Council before taking the Guild's leadership. The Guild also mounts the Artisan's Fair, a seasonal showcase of handcrafted goods, alchemical innovations, and magical inventions, which encourages collaboration between the Artisans' Council and the [[Order of Wisdom]].

## The District Around It

Aurora Song is the district where the contradictions of Aru'Mas are most visible, genuine creative energy on one street and sharp poverty on the next. Not all of its craft trade is legitimate. The [[Dragon's Claws]] maintain ties with black-market artisans, corrupt performers, and underground gambling operations in the district, and act as silent patrons of illegal dueling rings and smuggled enchanted instruments.

Mr. Vangrid P. Hershellman of [[Galvantis Arms, Armor, and Automatons]] is exploring collaborations with the inventors and artisans of the Aurora Song District, intending to join artistic work to mechanical engineering.
