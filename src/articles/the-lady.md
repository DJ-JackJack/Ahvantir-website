# The Lady

A veiled figure, narrow at the head and spreading to a hem, standing in the eastern half of the suns' road. The star at the top of her is the second brightest in the whole sky, and it is called **The Ladies Gaze**.

Nobody knows who she is.

## The figure

Five stars and five lines. Two strands run down from the head and apart, meeting again at the hem, so the shape closes — a narrow top opening into a wide bottom, which is a veil over a standing body and is very little else. The veil is swept to one side only. That asymmetry is doing more work than it looks: a symmetrical veil would read the same reflected, and a figure you cannot orient is a figure you cannot find twice.

She is forty-seven degrees tall, which is the same height as [[The Mother]]. For a second standing woman in the same sky that is reasonable company to keep, and it was not arranged — it is where the stars are.

Fourteen stars sit in her sign and five are used. The rest are left as field. She is tall rather than crowded.

The suns walk through her from **20 Keltharyn** to **9 Varenthal**, thirty-two days — which means the Lady is the figure the year turns inside. Whatever the old year was, it ends while the suns are in her, and the new one starts before they leave. She stands highest at midnight around **9 Embrathen**.

## Who addresses her

Three kinds of people, and they do not want the same thing.

**Thieves** make offerings in her name. Not elaborate ones: a coin left on a sill, a thread tied where it will not be noticed, the kind of observance that costs nothing and can be denied afterwards.

**Spies** whisper to be wrapped in her veil. The phrasing is fixed and it is always that phrasing, which suggests it came from somewhere rather than being invented over and over by people in the same trade.

**Those who walk the roads at night** pray to remain shielded from her sight.

The third is the odd one, and it is odd in a way worth sitting with. The first two ask her to hide them. The third asks to be hidden *from her*. She is both the thing that conceals and the thing that watches, and the people who address her do not appear to find this a difficulty.

## What is actually known

That she is veiled. That she is looking at you.

That is the whole of it. There is no story attached to her, no event she commemorates, no figure from the histories anyone has managed to attach her to with any confidence. She is not a mystery in the sense of a puzzle somebody is working on. She is a mystery in the sense that there is nothing to work on.

It would be easy to assume the explanation was lost, because a great deal was. But nothing supports that either. She may simply be a shape somebody saw, which got a character and never got a name to go with it, and which has been accumulating other people's business ever since.

## Why the gaze has a name

Of the five stars in her, one is named, and it is not her heart or her hands. It is her eyes.

The Ladies Gaze is magnitude −1.47 and the second brightest star in the sky. It climbs to eighty degrees from Aru'Mas, which is near enough overhead that finding it is not a skill. On a clear night it is one of the first things anyone notices, and it has been one of the first things anyone noticed for as long as there have been people here to notice it.

So the figure nobody can identify is built around the one part of her that is unmistakable, and that part is the part that is looking. Whatever she is, she was never hard to see. She was only ever hard to name.

The night travellers may have the better instinct. The thieves and the spies are asking a stranger for a favour. The ones on the road are at least taking seriously that something up there is paying attention.
