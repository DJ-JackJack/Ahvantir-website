# Marducian Calendar

The Marducian Calendar is the formal system of timekeeping used throughout [[Aru'Mas]] and most of the [[Nyhexus (The Material Plane)#The Ahvantir Archipelago|Ahvantir Archipelago]]. It was devised to accommodate the celestial conditions of the region, harmonising the planet's orbit around its twin suns, [[Solara and Nystara]], with the influence of its three moons — Miras, Toris and Keltas. It is at once a practical framework for civil and agricultural planning and a cultural structure for ritual observance and collective identity. Years are counted in the Marducian Calendar, abbreviated **MC**.

## The Year

A full Marducian year spans **386 days**: nine months totalling 384 days, plus two days that stand outside the weekly cycle. Six months run to forty-three days and three to forty-two. Every year opens on Solkir.

## The Turn

The weekly cycle is called a **Turn** and runs to eight days. Unlike calendars that align weeks to months, the Turn runs continuously across the year, interrupted only by the two days that fall outside it.

The first five days take their names from the five bodies of the sky, in order: Solara, Nystara, Miras, Toris and Keltas. Each weekday carries symbolic associations, and this rhythm underpins the functioning of markets, courts and councils, shaping economic and political life alike.

## The Days Outside the Turn

Two days each year fall outside the Turn entirely. Civic life pauses on both, and communities gather for ceremonies marking the seasonal transition.

**Cindrafel** — the burning's end. The blaze of Cindralis breaks and the harvest begins.

**Noctharis** — the night's keeping. A vigil of remembrance through the longest dark.

These were once the equinox observances, **Equinara** and **Umbrivale**, named for the days they marked. The calendar has drifted since. The two days keep their fixed places in the year, but no longer fall on the equinoxes, and now carry names fitted to where they actually land.

## The Moons

The calendar's solar structure provides the backbone for agricultural cycles, legal contracts and administrative planning. Its lunar dimension carries equal weight in spiritual and magical practice, and ritualists, farmers and mages alike layer the lunar rhythms over the solar calendar for guidance in planting, divination and spellwork.

| Moon | Cycle | Governs |
|---|---|---|
| **Miras** | 24 days | Change and emotional tides |
| **Toris** | 43 days, matching a long month | Labour and growth |
| **Keltas** | 66 days | Dreams, prophecy and death |

Because the three cycles share no common factor smaller than their product, the moons return to the same phases together only once every 11,352 days — close to twenty-nine and a half years.

## The Suns

[[Solara and Nystara]] orbit one another on a cycle of roughly eight Marducian years. At its close the two discs appear nearly adjacent, a convergence astronomers call **the Pairing**. The first recorded Pairing fell in 0 MC, and the count runs from there.
